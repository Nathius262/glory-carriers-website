Kabod’25: “Anticipation”

Join us for Kabod’25, a divine gathering designed to ignite revival and awaken the consciousness of God's glory across the nations. This global conference brings believers together in unity, revelation, and transformation — a time of outpouring and deep encounters with the Holy Spirit.

About the Conference

Kabod’25 aims to:

  • Deepen spiritual understanding — unveiling the dimensions of God’s glory and power.

  • Foster revival and growth — empowering believers to live as true carriers of divine presence.

  • Inspire transformation — equipping attendees to impact their world with the reality of Christ.

Who Should Attend?

Kabod’25 is open to everyone hungry for more of God — ministers, believers, leaders, and seekers desiring a deeper walk with the Spirit. Whether you’re new in faith or seasoned in ministry, this conference will be a defining moment in your spiritual journey.

Date and Location

  • Date: 27–29 November, 2025

  • Location: Sheraton Hotel, Makurdi, Benue State

We look forward to welcoming you to Kabod’25 — where His glory shall be revealed.
