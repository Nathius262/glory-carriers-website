Zoe Conference 2025: "New Wine"

Join us for the annual Zoe Conference, a global gathering dedicated to spreading the Gospel and transforming lives through the power of God's word. This year's edition takes place in May, bringing together people from around the world for a time of revelation, renewal, and spiritual growth.

About the Conference

Zoe Conference aims to:

  • Deepen spiritual understanding: Explore the depths of God's word and its application in our lives.

  • Foster spiritual growth: Provide a platform for believers to grow in their faith and relationship with God.

  • Inspire and motivate: Equip attendees with the tools and inspiration needed to live a life that reflects God's love and purpose.

Who Should Attend?

Zoe Conference is open to people of all ages and backgrounds who are seeking a deeper relationship with God and a fresh perspective on life. Whether you're a long-time believer or just starting your spiritual journey, this conference promises to be a valuable experience.

Date and Location

  • Date: 29-31 May, 2025

  • Location: Sheraton Hotel Makurdi, Benue State

We look forward to welcoming you to the Zoe Conference 2025.
